# Sprint Presentation Guide

## 1. Product Backlog

| ID | Feature | User Story | Priority | Estimation |
|----|---------|------------|----------|------------|
| 1 | Authentication | 1/ As a User, I can authenticate using my email and password to access my role-specific interface. | High | 4 Days |
| 2 | Profile management | 2/ As a User, I can manage my account through updating personal information (full name, email, phone number) and password. | Medium | 3 Days |
| 3 | User management | 3/1 As an Administrator, I can consult, search, and filter users by role. <br>3/2 As an Administrator, I can add, activate, deactivate, and delete users from the system. | High | 5 Days |
| 4 | Notification management | 4/1 As a User, I can receive notifications related to activities and workflow events concerning my role within the platform. <br>4/2 As a User, I can consult notification history and track unread notifications. <br>4/3 As a User, I can perform actions that trigger notifications for other users according to the platform workflow. | Medium | 5 Days |
| 5 | Admin dashboard | 5/1 As an Administrator, I can consult a dashboard summarizing the sample pipeline, evaluation workflow states, and purchase decisions. <br>5/2 As an Administrator, I can monitor sample processing and pending decisions in real time. <br>5/3 As an Administrator, I can analyze collector, supplier, and stock performance through charts, statistics, and KPI indicators. <br>5/4 As an Administrator, I can view the interactive geographic coverage of collectors on a map. | Medium | 7 Days |
| 6 | Sample consultation | 6/1 As an Administrator, I can consult detailed sample information and search or filter samples by date, status, and sample information grouped by collectors. <br>6/2 As an Administrator, I can consult collectors' geographic coverage through maps. | High | 6 Days |
| 7 | Organoleptic analysis consultation | 7/1 As an Administrator, I can consult detailed sample information and search or filter samples by date, status, and sample information. <br>7/2 As an Administrator, I can consult detailed tasting evaluations submitted by panel members for each sample. <br>7/3 As an Administrator, I can approve or refuse purchases and mark samples as urgent to notify panel members to prioritize evaluations for that sample. | High | 7 Days |
| 8 | Laboratory analysis consultation | 8/1 As an Administrator, I can consult detailed sample information and search or filter samples by date, status, and sample information. <br>8/2 As an Administrator, I can consult detailed laboratory analysis reports for each sample. | High | 6 Days |
| 9 | Confirmed purchases management | 9/1 As an Administrator, I can consult detailed sample information and search or filter samples by date, status, and sample information. <br>9/2 As an Administrator, I can consult detailed confirmed purchase information. <br>9/3 As an Administrator, I can approve confirmed purchases and define the desired stock delivery date. | High | 6 Days |
| 10 | Evaluation dashboard | 10/1 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult my evaluation pipeline, workflow states, and urgent evaluations. <br>10/2 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult my attendance statistics for tasting sessions. <br>10/3 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can analyze submission delays, classifications, and recent activities through charts and statistics. <br>10/4 As a Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult and approve or refuse tasting sessions proposed by panel members. <br>10/5 As a Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can monitor panel members' submission delays and performance statistics. <br>10/6 As a Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can analyze panel alignment percentages and detect evaluation divergences between panel members. <br>10/7 As a Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult global panel classifications and supervision activities through charts and statistics. | Medium | 7 Days |
| 11 | Sample management | 11/1 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult detailed sample information and search or filter samples by date, status, and sample information. <br>11/2 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can add, edit, and delete samples, and extract bottle information from handwritten labels using AI-assisted OCR. <br>11/3 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can confirm the physical reception of a sample at the company so that the evaluation workflow can continue. | High | 14 Days |
| 12 | Sample evaluation | 12/1 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult detailed sample information and search or filter samples according to evaluation workflow states, dates, and sample information. <br>12/2 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can start, continue, consult previously submitted evaluations, save evaluation drafts, and submit evaluations to lock them from further modification. <br>12/3 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can evaluate samples by filling the evaluation form. | High | 9 Days |
| 13 | Laboratory analysis consultation | 13/1 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult detailed sample information and search or filter samples by date, status, and sample information. <br>13/2 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult laboratory analysis reports and send urgent requests to prioritize laboratory analyses for certain samples. | Medium | 5 Days |
| 14 | Tasting session management | 14/1 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult detailed tasting session information and search or filter tasting sessions by date, status, and session information. <br>14/2 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can create, edit, and delete tasting sessions that I organized and confirm attendance for tasting sessions where I am invited. <br>14/3 As a Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can approve or refuse tasting sessions proposed by panel members. | High | 8 Days |
| 15 | Panel members consultation | 15/1 As a Panel Member / Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult and search panel members. | Low | 2 Days |
| 16 | Cross-panel evaluation supervision | 16/1 As a Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult detailed sample information and search or filter samples by date, status, and sample information. <br>16/2 As a Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can consult side-by-side evaluations submitted by panel members for each sample. <br>16/3 As a Panel Evaluation Supervisor, I can use AI-assisted divergence detection to identify evaluations that differ significantly from the rest of the panel. | High | 8 Days |
| 17 | Sample management | 17/1 As a Collector, I can consult detailed sample information, negotiation details, and purchase order details and search or filter samples by date, status, and sample information. <br>17/2 As a Collector, I can add, update, and delete samples and extract bottle information from handwritten labels using AI-assisted OCR, all according to sample workflow restrictions. <br>17/3 As a Collector, I can confirm purchases by submitting negotiation and stock delivery information after the sample is approved by the administrator. | High | 10 Days |
| 18 | Interactive geographic coverage consultation | 18/1 As a Collector, I can consult the interactive geographic coverage of my collection activities through maps based on governorates and delegations associated with registered samples. | Medium | 4 Days |
| 19 | Laboratory analysis management | 19/1 As a Laboratory Technician, I can consult detailed sample information and search or filter samples by date, status, and sample information. <br>19/2 As a Laboratory Technician, I can update and delete laboratory analysis reports according to the analysis workflow and sample processing status. <br>19/3 As a Laboratory Technician, I can manually add laboratory analysis reports by entering laboratory result values into a structured analysis form. <br>19/4 As a Laboratory Technician, I can scan paper laboratory reports so that AI-assisted OCR automatically extracts and fills laboratory analysis values. | High | 10 Days |
